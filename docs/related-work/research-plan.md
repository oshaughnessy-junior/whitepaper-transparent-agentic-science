# Related-work research foundation

## Question and decision

Which existing research systems, evaluation methods, reproducibility practices and practitioner observations most directly ground or qualify this whitepaper's claims about reliable agentic science? Deliver a source-checked evidence map, a reusable-method radar, and gentle citation/prior-work additions with the fuller comparison in an appendix, preserving the accepted title, abstract and argument.

## Fixed evidence questions

- What has actually been demonstrated in agentic astronomy and broader scientific research, versus proposed in architectures, talks or promotional posts?
- How do verification, independent review, production tests, provenance and human scientific judgment feature in those demonstrations?
- What supports or qualifies the distinction between bug finding, conducting an experiment, making a scientific inference and writing a coherent paper?
- What gaps and benchmark limitations prevent extrapolation to a general frontier-model-versus-human performance claim?
- Which primary talks, posts and project documents capture recent work not yet represented by a paper?

## Search boundary

Public primary sources available by 2026-10-25: author/lab pages, official talks/slides, research papers and their official projects/code, original empirical evaluations, substantive first-party practitioner posts and standards. Start with Yuan-Sen Ting's Ohio State homepage, talks, CASPER and collaborators, then follow relevant backward and forward links. Secondary indexes aid discovery only. No private messages, unpublished research or local runtime records enter the report. Capture dates/versions and retrieval limitations; distinguish latest public claims from reproduced evidence.

## Method questions

Extract operational practices for independent replication, held-out verification, benchmark validity, bounded context/editing, result logging, and readable evidence-backed narratives. Record observed inputs/transformation/outputs, constraints and cheapest validation experiment. This research does not adopt a new production method or assert field novelty.

## Execution and gates

Root researcher handles Ting/astronomy, source checking and synthesis. Three bounded worker lanes examine autonomous-science systems, verification/provenance/evaluation, and recent primary practitioner reports. Maximum active agents: four including root. Each worker returns a compact JSON evidence packet (at most eight extracts, four method candidates) plus artifact paths, gaps and contradictions. Synthesis follows source/extraction review; manuscript claims require source identity and semantic support. No automatic method adoption or broad manuscript rewrite is authorized by this research task.

## Success and exclusions

Cover the main nearby systems and the strongest counterevidence with real primary sources; identify must-cite work and useful emerging/non-paper sources; explain how each changes this guide's positioning. Preserve the accepted title/abstract and existing prose outside bounded additions. Keep detailed survey material separate from the readable paper. Do not infer scientific validity from publication, a site badge, a model score or a source-code repository. No new scientific campaigns, external messages, production changes or submissions.

## Human steering of scope

Keep main-text changes gentle. The more aggressive autonomous-research programs, especially CS/ML systems with executable evaluation targets, are a comparison class rather than this guide's objective. Differentiate task, domain, human role and evidence of success; do not dismiss their results or turn this practice guide into an autonomous-science proposal.
