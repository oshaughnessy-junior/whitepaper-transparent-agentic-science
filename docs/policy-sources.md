# Official policy sources for the scientific introduction

Read on 2026-10-23. The manuscript cites the original policies, not summaries of penalties in inherited skills.

- [Physical Review / APS AI policy](https://journals.aps.org/authors/appropriate-use-ai-tools): substantive-use disclosure and author verification; identifies the information a disclosure should contain.
- [PRD author guidance](https://journals.aps.org/prd/authors): links the APS policy and REVTeX preparation guidance.
- [ApJ / AAS author guidance](https://journals.aas.org/author-llm-guidelines): supervised manuscript preparation, disclosure, verification and literature checks. This is manuscript guidance, not a general research-method prohibition. The [September announcement](https://journals.aas.org/news/new-guidelines-on-ai-use-in-aas-journals/) describes implementation for new submissions later in the fall; avoid inventing a precise effective date.
- [arXiv generative-AI guidance](https://info.arxiv.org/help/moderation/index.html#policy-for-authors-use-of-generative-ai-language-tools): report significant use, retain author responsibility, and do not list AI tools as authors. No universal fixed suspension term is asserted from this page.

The second introduction paragraph summarizes the shared concerns. The subsequent process recommendations are this guide's response, not additional requirements attributed to a venue. Final human approval and a complete model/version disclosure remain necessary before submission.
