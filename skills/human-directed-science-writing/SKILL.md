# Human Directed Science Writing

Begin with a human scientific brief: question, contribution, audience, structure, claim strength, required evidence, terminology and exclusions. If it is incomplete, draft only supported material and list consequential open decisions. Do not infer novelty from effort or a successful run.

Keep chronological logs, durable scientific notes, current interpretation, and manuscript distinct. Establish evidence in notes, then organize the paper by claim. Develop the formalism and define symbols before implementation details. Abstract and conclusion must not exceed the evidence in the body.

Every computed quantity uses generated result keys. Anchor major quantitative claims to evidence-bearing figures or tables. Verify bibliographic identity and read the passage supporting each attribution. A real reference can still support the wrong claim.

Let the human guide the thesis, high-level organization and section roles. Draft bounded subjects first. Preserve an accepted title and abstract unless their revision is explicitly requested. Work section by section in bounded revisions; name the defect and the content to preserve before editing. An instruction to tighten or clarify does not authorize a replacement argument. Validate each edit against the accepted baseline with a redline diff, checking intent, emphasis, claim strength and evidence as well as wording. Propose broader restructuring separately for human review. Work in bounded revisions. Remove inflated rhetoric, repeated caveats, notebook chronology, and unsupported certainty. Preserve numbers, units, inequalities, uncertainty definitions, citations, equations and scope during stylistic editing; report any needed scientific change separately. Have the human settle the thesis and final claim strength.

Before handoff, compile, inspect the rendered artifact, check result provenance, audit source/comments and references, and report remaining gates accurately. Submission rules are venue-specific and must be verified from current official sources. See ../../docs/workflow.md.

Repeat result and retraction checks after the final human or collaborator edit; human involvement does not make an edit immune to staleness. Preserve the human's qualified wording and treat physical intuition as a hypothesis to test, not an instruction to manufacture agreement. See ../../docs/cardassia-lessons.md.
