# Bounded independent review

Supply the exact scientific claim, candidate identity, domain, units, assumptions, acceptance conditions, raw evidence locations and authorized resources. Withhold the implementer's advocacy and other reviewers' conclusions on the first pass. Necessary assumptions are not withheld for artificial blindness.

The science reviewer attempts an independent derivation or counterexample, tests whether the inference follows, and identifies shared assumptions or missing physical tests. The code/numerics reviewer traces the real entry point, checks conditioning, boundaries, effective settings, convergence, test collection and a relevant negative control in isolation. Neither changes the candidate or reads the other's first report.

Record reviewer identity and exposure, actual commands/derivations, reference lineage, tested domain, findings and scope-limited verdict. Append each finding's disposition without erasing the objection. Pin final revisions and recheck affected results after repairs. Review spent tuning the candidate is not fresh confirmation. Record missing review explicitly.

This contract adapts the review discipline of agent-skills and its attributed BootLoops influences; it imports no private project records or upstream BootLoops implementation. See ../../../PROVENANCE.md and ../../../NOTICE.md.
